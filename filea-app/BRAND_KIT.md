# 🧘 BRAND KIT — "Filea"

> Daily stretch, yoga & mobility routines · Age rating 3+ / Everyone
> Reference / benchmark app: **Bend** (Bend is the inspiration, not the product).

---

## 1. Brand Foundation

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Filea |
| **Category** | Health & Fitness → Stretching, Mobility & Yoga |
| **Age rating** | 3+ / Everyone (safe, non-violent, no risky content) |
| **One-liner** | *Move better every day — guided stretch & mobility in minutes.* |
| **Mission** | Help anyone restore and keep their body's natural range of motion, with short daily routines that adapt to how they feel. |
| **Positioning** | The *adaptive* mobility companion — calmer than gym apps, smarter than a static stretch library. |
| **Twist vs. reference (Bend)** | Bend gives you a library; **Filea asks how you feel and picks the right routine for today** (adaptive daily check-in). |

### Name
**Filea** ✅ — chosen by the founder. Soft, feminine-flowing sound; evokes *fil* (thread/line — the body as one continuous line that stretches and flows) and *ease*. Short, ownable, works internationally.

### Brand Personality
- **Calm** — never shouty, no "crush your goals" hustle culture
- **Approachable** — beginner-safe, zero intimidation
- **Trustworthy** — grounded, physio-adjacent credibility
- **Minimal** — clean, uncluttered, breathable design
- **Quietly smart** — adapts to you without making a fuss about it

### Voice & Tone
- Warm, encouraging, second person: *"Let's loosen those hips."*
- Short sentences. Gentle verbs: *ease, release, unlock, restore, unwind.*
- Never guilt-based. Celebrate consistency, not intensity.

## 2. Logo & Symbol

- **Wordmark:** "filea" in lowercase, rounded geometric sans — soft curves echo flexibility.
- **Symbol:** A single continuous line that arcs/bends into an open loop (a body easing into a forward fold). Works standalone as the app-icon mark.
- **App icon:** Rounded-corner tile, sage → teal gradient background, single off-white curved-line mark centered.
- **Clear space:** Minimum padding = height of the "f" on all sides.

## 4. Typography

| Use | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display / Headings | **Fraunces** or **Recoleta** | Soft serif, warm & premium |
| Body / UI | **Inter** or **DM Sans** | Clean, highly legible |
| Timers / Numbers | **DM Sans (tabular figures)** | Countdown clarity |

**Type scale (mobile):** H1 32 / H2 24 / H3 20 / Body 16 / Caption 13
**Line-height:** 1.5 (generous, calm feel)

---

## 5. Iconography & Imagery

- **Icons:** Rounded, 2px stroke, single-weight line icons. The ⊕ motif is the visual bullet/tag for routines.
- **Illustration:** Simple flat human figures mid-stretch, warm neutral tones, no faces (inclusive & universal).
- **Photography (optional):** Soft natural light, home/floor settings, muted tones — *never* gym mirrors or heavy sweat.
- **Motion:** Gentle looping pose animations; ease-in-out only, no snappy transitions. Everything breathes.

---

## 6. UX Principles

1. **Anytime, anywhere** — no equipment, works in small spaces.
2. **Zero-friction start** — one tap from home into a routine.
3. **No pressure** — gentle streaks, not aggressive goals.
4. **Guided calm** — voice/visual timers, soothing audio cues.
5. **Accessible (3+)** — large touch targets, high-contrast option, simple language.

## 7. Routine Categories (content pillars)

| ⊕ Category | Promise |
|---|---|
| **Wake Up** | Quick daily routine to maintain natural mobility & range of motion — anytime, anywhere. |
| **Posture Reset** | Seated stretches that correct habitual posture problems (shoulders, back, neck). |
| **Full Body** | 20+ stretches targeting key muscles and joints across the whole body. |
| **Sleep** | Gentle long-hold stretches to unwind, de-stress, and improve sleep quality. |
| **Expert** | Advanced stretches & yoga poses covering all major muscle groups and extremities. |
| **Hips** | Deep, focused stretches to unlock tight hips and undo hours of sitting. |
| **Hamstrings** | Subtle stretches to reduce tightness and relieve knees, pelvis, lower back. |
| **Lower Back** | Gentle stretches to reduce and prevent lower back pain. |
| **Isometric** | Static-contraction routines building strength, balance, and range of motion. |
| **Create Your Own** | Custom routines from a library of hundreds of stretches, poses & isometric exercises. |
